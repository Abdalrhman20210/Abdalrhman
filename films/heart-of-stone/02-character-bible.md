# دليل الشخصيات – قلب من حجر

> **طريقة العمل في Google Flow:**
> 1. لكل شخصية: افتح «الشخصيات» ← شخصية جديدة ← الصق **برومبت الصورة المرجعية** في خانة «ما هي التغييرات المطلوبة؟» (النموذج Nano Banana).
> 2. سمِّ الشخصية **بالاسم المكتوب** بالضبط، لأن صفحة البرومبتات تستخدم هذه الأسماء.
> 3. الصق **معلومات الشخصية** في خانتها، واترك «اختيار صوت» فارغاً، ثم اضغط «تم».
> 4. البلوكات التي تبدأ بـ `[HAJAR]` وأمثالها هي الوصف الثابت الذي يدخل تلقائياً في برومبتات الفيديو. لا تحتاج تنسخها.

---

## 🎨 الستايل العام

```
STYLE: photorealistic cinematic film still, epic fantasy drama in a Levantine mountain valley, earthy stone and olive tones with warm golden light, deep blue-grey shadows, volumetric light, soft haze, natural realistic lighting, clean digital cinema image, full frame with no borders, shot on ARRI Alexa, 16:9
```

```
NEGATIVE: film border, frame numbers, text, watermark, logo, cartoon, anime, plastic look, extra fingers, deformed hands, modern clothing, cars, power lines, blurry face
```

---

## 🪨 حَجَر (البطل)

### 1. اسم الشخصية في Flow: `حجر`

**برومبت الصورة المرجعية:**
```
character reference sheet, three views side by side on a plain neutral grey background: full body front view, full body side view, and close-up of the face. A gentle 4-meter-tall giant made of weathered grey-brown valley stones and boulders fitted together like a dry stone wall, patches of green moss and small lichens, a broad heavy face with a big stone brow, two round eyes glowing with soft warm amber light, a small hollow niche in the center of his chest holding a single bright yellow wildflower, huge rounded hands, a kind and shy expression. Photorealistic, cinematic soft light, highly detailed stone texture, no text.
```

**معلومات الشخصية:**
```
Hajar, a gentle stone giant carved by an old sculptor. Moves slowly and carefully so he never breaks anything. Shy, kind, silent, never fights back. His amber eyes glow brighter when he is happy and dim when he is sad. Always keeps a yellow wildflower in the niche in his chest.
```

**الوصف الثابت (يدخل في برومبتات الفيديو):**
```
[HAJAR] a gentle 4-meter-tall giant made of weathered grey-brown valley stones fitted together, patches of green moss, a broad heavy stone face, two round eyes glowing soft warm amber, a small niche in the center of his chest holding a bright yellow wildflower, huge rounded stone hands
```

**حَجَر المتكسّر (المشهد 4، نفس الشخصية `حجر`):**
```
[HAJAR-BROKEN] the same gentle stone giant, now cracked and crumbling, chunks of stone missing from his shoulders and arms, soaked with water, the amber glow in his eyes very faint, the yellow wildflower still in his chest niche
```
